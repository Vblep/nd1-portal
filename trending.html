ConNovate 2025

19th International Conference on Technology and Computing

Theme: Intelligent, Secure, and Sustainable Innovations for Connected World

Date: August 11th – 14th, 2025

Location: Kano, Nigeria

About ConNovate 2025

Nigeria Computer Society presents a prestigious gathering that brings together thought leaders, researchers, innovators, and policy makers to explore cutting-edge solutions addressing digital challenges. This year’s theme centers on digital innovation, cybersecurity, sustainability, and connected systems.

Focal Areas:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics
  • Green Computing and Sustainability
  • Cloud Computing and Big Data
  • Financial Technology (FinTech)
  • Human-Centered Computing
  • Emerging Technologies
  • Software Engineering
  • Data Science and Analytics
